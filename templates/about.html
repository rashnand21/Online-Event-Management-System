About EventPro

An online event management system built to make event planning and booking simple.

What is EventPro?

EventPro is a web-based event management system. Organizers create events, schedules, packages and offers. Users browse events, book seats, choose halls and pay online. Admins manage halls, services, payments and feedback from one place.

The goal is to replace manual processes with a simple, fast and reliable platform for in-person events of any size.

Our mission

To save time and money for organizers, increase attendance, and give every attendee a smooth booking experience.

Key features

Event management

Create events with dates, locations, capacity and booking deadlines.

Packages and offers

Offer packages and discount codes for each event.

Hall and slot booking

Choose from available halls and reserve time slots.

Online payments

Pay by UPI, card or cash and track every payment.

Staff and schedule

Assign staff and publish a clear activity schedule.

Feedback and ratings

Collect ratings and comments after every event.

Who uses EventPro?

Users

Browse events, view schedules, book seats, pay, cancel bookings and give feedback.

Organizers

Create events, add schedules, packages, offers and staff, and track bookings.

Admins

Manage halls, services, events, payments and feedback across the system.
